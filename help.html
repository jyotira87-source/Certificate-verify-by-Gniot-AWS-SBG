How to Verify a Certificate

  1. Go to the Verify Certificate page.
  2. Enter the certificate ID printed on the certificate.
  3. Click Verify to check against the official registry.
  4. Or use Scan Certificate to scan with your camera.

Understanding Results

  • Verified: Certificate is authentic and active.
  • Expired: Certificate is authentic but past its expiry date.
  • Revoked: Certificate has been revoked by the issuer.
  • Not found: Certificate ID is not in the registry.

For Administrators

  • Log in via Admin Login to register and manage certificates.
  • View audit logs for all administrative actions.
  • Export records as CSV for reporting.
