Privacy Policy

Advance Certificate Verification respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We only collect certificate metadata that you submit for registration or verification (holder name, title, organization, dates, certificate code). We do not store uploaded certificate images or extracted OCR text unless explicitly required and protected by a documented retention policy.

Verification Logs

Verification attempts are logged with certificate ID, outcome, and timestamp for audit purposes. No sensitive personal information is stored in public logs.

Your Rights

You may request correction or deletion of your certificate records by contacting the issuing organization.
